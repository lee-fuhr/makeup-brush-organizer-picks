How to choose a makeup brush organizer

Updated June 2026Independently researchedNo paid placement.

Quick answer

Choosing a makeup brush organizer comes down to matching your storage space, brush collection size, and cleaning habits. Prioritize an open-air design (like acrylic or bamboo cups) for everyday access, make sure the compartments fit your brush handles, and pick a material that won’t harbor bacteria or break easily.

What type of makeup brush organizer should I get?

The most common styles are countertop cups, rotating stands, drawer inserts, and travel rolls. Countertop cups (often made of clear acrylic or natural bamboo) are ideal if you use your brushes every day and want quick, visible access. Rotating stands save space by letting you spin through your collection, but they can be top-heavy if you have many long-handled brushes. Drawer inserts are a smart choice if you prefer a clutter-free counter. They keep brushes lying flat inside a drawer, protected from dust and sunlight. Travel rolls or cases are best for people who frequently move their brushes, though they don’t offer the same display or airflow as open organizers.

How do I know the right size and capacity?

Start by counting how many brushes you own and noting their handle lengths. Most organizers work well for standard brush handles (roughly finger-length to pencil-length), but extra-long handles from professional brands may not fit tall cups or compartments. If you have a collection of 8–12 brushes, a simple cup with 3–4 compartments is usually enough. For larger collections (20+ brushes), look for carousel-style organizers or multi-tier stands. Just be honest about your space: a bulky organizer on a tiny bathroom vanity can feel cramped, while a too-small cup on a large counter can look bare.

Which material is best: acrylic, bamboo, ceramic, or metal?

Acrylic is the most popular because it’s transparent (you see every brush), lightweight, and easy to clean with soap and water. The downside? Lower-cost acrylic can scratch or yellow over time, so look for thick, crystal-clear acrylic if possible. Bamboo offers a warm, natural look and is durable, but it’s porous, so it can absorb moisture and bacteria if you store wet brushes. Ceramic and metal (often with a painted finish) are heavier and feel more upscale, but they can chip or rust if not well-made. For a mix of style and practicality, many people choose a bamboo base with acrylic compartments.

Should I get a closed container or an open-air holder?

Open-air holders (cups with holes or slots) allow air to circulate around your brush bristles, which helps them dry fully between uses and reduces bacteria growth. Closed containers, such as zippered pouches or boxes with lids, protect brushes from dust and sunlight but can trap moisture, especially if you store brushes before they’re completely dry. For daily use, an open-air holder is almost always the better choice. Keep a closed container only for travel or long-term storage of brushes you rarely use. If you’re worried about dust, a simple dust cover (like a pretty cloth) over an open stand works well without trapping moisture.

How do I keep my brush organizer clean?

Clean your organizer at least once a month to prevent makeup residue and germs from building up. For acrylic or glass, a quick wipe with a damp microfiber cloth and a drop of dish soap is sufficient. Bamboo should be wiped sparingly, never soaked, and dried immediately to avoid warping. Avoid using harsh chemicals like alcohol or bleach on porous materials, as they can damage the finish. Also, make a habit of letting brushes dry bristle-down in a cup or on a rack before returning them to the organizer. This simple step extends the life of both your brushes and the holder.

Frequently asked questions

Can I store makeup brushes in a cup with the bristles facing down?

Yes, but it’s best for dry, clean brushes only. Storing brushes bristle-down can bend the ferrule (the metal part) over time if the cup is too deep, and it can trap moisture if the brushes are still damp. For everyday storage, bristles-up is generally safer.

How often should I wash my brush organizer?

Aim for once a month, or more often if you notice dust or product buildup. Acrylic and glass holders can be washed with warm, soapy water, while bamboo should be wiped, never submerged.

What’s the best organizer for travel?

A roll-up fabric case or a rigid zip case with elastic slots is ideal. Look for one that fits your brush lengths and has a waterproof lining to protect against spills. Avoid glass or heavy ceramic organizers for travel.

How do I stop my brushes from falling over in a cup?

Use a cup with compartments or fill the bottom with decorative beads, glass pebbles, or coffee beans to prop handles upright. Just make sure the filler is clean and non-absorbent, or replace it regularly to prevent mold.

Is it okay to store wet makeup brushes in an organizer?

No. Wet brushes should never be placed in a closed or semi-closed organizer because trapped moisture encourages mold and bacteria growth. Always let brushes dry completely, bristles pointing down, on a towel or a dedicated drying rack before returning them to storage.

In shortThe best makeup brush organizer fits your routine, not just your counter. Focus on open-air designs for daily use, choose a material you’re willing to clean, and match the size to your actual brush collection. Skip the bells and whistles if they compromise ventilation or stability. A simple, well-made organizer that keeps your brushes dry and visible will serve you better than anything fancy.
