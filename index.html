How we choose our picks

We don’t test products in a lab or run timed trials. Instead, we research picks based on three things: the brand’s reputation among beauty communities, long-term owner feedback from verified purchasers (especially comments from people who’ve had the holder for six months or more), and published expert reviews from sources that take a critical look at materials and build quality. Our focus is on what actually breaks or frustrates people over time, acrylic that crazes or cracks, bases that tip when loaded unevenly, and designs that trap water or makeup residue. We favor organizers that survive daily use, clean up easily, and don’t sacrifice stability for looks.
